    How to spot a fake QR code

    • Sticker on top of the real code. Look closely at payment QR codes on counters and parking meters. A sticker pasted over the original is the most common attack.
    • The address does not match the place. A coffee shop should link to a coffee shop domain, not "pay-now-secure.xyz". Read the destination before you open it.
    • Payment requests hidden in a link. Some QR codes encode a UPI or payment link directly. You can accept or reject payment prompts in your app, but read the amount and recipient first.
    • Pressure in the message. "Scan now to claim your prize", "Your parcel is held — scan to release it." Urgency is a manipulation tactic, not a reason to hurry.
    • Bank and government websites. Type the address yourself. Never rely on a QR code, a text, or a link for your banking app, tax portal or any login page that matters.
